## 4.1 Baking a Cake
My pseudo code for baking a cake is as follows...

Pre-heat oven to 400 degrees

Mix 3 eggs, 2/3 cup of sugar, 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract, and a pinch of salt in a bowl using a whisk

Pour in 1/2 cup of vegetable oil and continue whisking 

Add in 1 cup of flour, 1 teaspoon of baking powder, and 1.4 teaspoon of baking soda and mix with spatual

Slowly add in 1/4 cup of milk and continue mixing 

Pour contents into a pan and bake for around 40 minutes 

After 40 minutes place a knife into pan

if knife comes out clean 

  take cake out of oven and leave to cool
  
else: 

  leave cake in for another 5 minutes and check again 
  
Enjoy!
